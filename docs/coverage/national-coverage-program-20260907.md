# Programa nacional de cobertura — 2026-09-07

O planejamento normativo está em
[SDD 0078](../../specs/changes/0078-national-coverage-lawful-access/spec-of-specs.md).

Subprogramas:

- [0079 — registro nacional](../../specs/changes/0079-national-surface-registry-reconciliation/spec.md)
- [0080 — acesso público legítimo](../../specs/changes/0080-legitimate-public-access-runtime/spec.md)
- [0081 — primeiro grau estadual](../../specs/changes/0081-state-first-degree-expansion/spec.md)
- [0082 — famílias trabalhista/eleitoral](../../specs/changes/0082-national-labor-electoral-families/spec.md)
- [0083 — federal/superior/militar](../../specs/changes/0083-federal-superior-military-closure/spec.md)
- [0084 — campos e inteiro teor](../../specs/changes/0084-fulltext-and-field-completeness/spec.md)
- [0085 — certificação contínua](../../specs/changes/0085-continuous-provider-certification/spec.md)

O pacote não declara cobertura concluída. A implementação futura deverá
regenerar o baseline, manter bloqueios explícitos e não executar bypass de
CAPTCHA, WAF, login, rate limit ou TLS.

O guia operacional de técnicas permitidas está em
[legitimate-techniques.md](../../specs/changes/0078-national-coverage-lawful-access/legitimate-techniques.md).
