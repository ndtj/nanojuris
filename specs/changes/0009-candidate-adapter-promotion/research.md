# Research

## Evidence

The local discovery snapshot observed public UI/catalog responses for nine
catalog candidates. Four sources had HTTP-valid observations, but the valid
responses were shells, forms or catalog surfaces rather than reproducible
decision-result payloads.

The SJUR/TSE dossier separately documents four public metadata routes with a
stable catalog contract: `classes`, `relatorias`, `eleicoes` and `normas`.
Those routes can be promoted as `catalog_only`; decision search cannot.

## Boundary

TJMG, TJRN and TRF3 remain candidates because their local valid observations do
not contain a reproducible result schema, parser fixture or stable pagination/
detail contract. No adapter is promoted for them in this change.
